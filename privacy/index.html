Cozy Cafe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Agentik Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Cozy Cafe (the "Game"). Please read it to understand what information is involved, why, and what choices you have.

In short: the Game has no accounts and we do not run servers that collect your personal data. Your progress stays on your device. Ads are shown by Google AdMob and purchases are processed by Google Play; those Google services handle the data they need under Google's own policies.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored only on your device

Your game progress and settings — level, coins, gems, hearts, boosters, collected recipes, chosen language, sound and vibration settings, and similar game data — are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

1.2 Information collected by our service providers

The Game uses Google AdMob to show ads and Google Play to process purchases (see Sections 3 and 4). These services may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (country or region) may be derived;
  • device and system information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type;
  • information about your interaction with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostic data about ad delivery;
  • purchase information: which product was bought, order number and purchase confirmation.

This information is collected and processed by Google as described in Google's policies. We do not receive your payment card details.

1.3 Information you send us

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and everything you include in your message. When you use "Contact us" in the Game, the email is pre-filled with the Game version, device model, operating system version, language and your current level so that we can help you faster; you can delete any of it before sending.

1.4 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, phone number, postal address, photos, contacts, precise location or account credentials, and the Game has no sign-in, chat, social or user-generated content features.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the Game: saving your progress and settings on your device.
  • To show ads: AdMob uses the information described above to select, deliver, limit and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud.
  • To deliver purchases: Google Play confirms a purchase so that the Game can give you the item and restore permanent purchases.
  • To provide support: we use your email and message only to answer you, fix problems and improve the Game.
  • To comply with law: where required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is supported by ads: a banner in the menu, optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between levels (never in the middle of a level). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID and your activity in other apps and websites.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • The in-app purchase "No ads" removes the menu banner and the ads between levels. Rewarded videos stay available but are always optional.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, AdMob and user data, Google ad settings.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play)

All purchases in the Game (gem packs, "No ads", "Unlimited hearts") are made through Google Play. Google processes your payment and payment details under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. We receive only the information needed to deliver and verify the purchase (product, order number, purchase status).

5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information for money and we do not share it with anyone, except:

  • Service providers: Google (AdMob and Google Play), as described above;
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, a court order or a valid request of a public authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of users, the public or us;
  • Business transfer: if the Game is transferred to another owner, the new owner must keep following this policy;
  • With your consent.

Under some US state laws, allowing personalized advertising may be considered "sharing" or a "sale" of personal information. You can opt out at any time as described in Section 3 or by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they process:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (ad consent in the EEA/UK/Switzerland) — Google Privacy Policy

7. Data Retention

  • Game progress is kept on your device until you delete the Game or its data.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable period afterwards (normally no longer than 24 months), then deleted. You can ask us to delete them earlier.
  • Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

8. Security

Game data is stored in the Game's private storage on your device and in-game balances are protected against simple tampering. Communication with Google services is encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle.

9. International Transfers

Google may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using the safeguards described in its policies (such as standard contractual clauses).

10. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children under the age of digital consent in their country should play with a parent's or guardian's permission, and purchases should be made by or with an adult. Parents can enable purchase authentication in Google Play. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Brazil or certain US states), you may have the right to: access the personal information we hold about you; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to its processing; receive it in a portable format; withdraw consent at any time; opt out of personalized advertising ("sale"/"sharing" under US state laws); and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we do not keep game data about you on our side, most requests are fulfilled by deleting the Game's data on your device or through the Google tools in Section 3. For anything else — including support emails — write to agentikstudiosupport@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days (45 days where the law allows). We may ask you to confirm your request from the same email address. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with the subject "Appeal".

Legal bases (EEA/UK): keeping your progress and delivering purchases — performance of our agreement with you; personalized ads — your consent; non-personalized ads, security and fraud prevention — our legitimate interests in offering a free, ad-supported game; support — your request and our legitimate interests.
